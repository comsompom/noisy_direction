# Image Generation Prompts: NoiseRadar

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Use these prompts to generate visual assets for NoiseRadar. Keep all output clean, original, non-photorealistic unless specified, and free of copyrighted brands, app-store badges, Apple logos, device mockups, or misleading emergency/security claims.

## 1. App Icon Prompt

Target use:
- iOS app icon.
- Export final as PNG, 1024 x 1024 px.
- No text.
- Must remain readable at small sizes.

Prompt:

```text
Create a polished iOS app icon for an app named NoiseRadar, a sound direction scanner. The icon should show a clean circular radar interface combined with a stylized sound wave and compass direction cue. Use a dark charcoal or deep graphite background, with a bright green acoustic wave/radar pulse and a small warm red directional marker pointing toward the strongest sound. The design should feel modern, technical, trustworthy, and useful for outdoor sound detection. Use simple bold shapes, high contrast, subtle depth, and a centered composition. No words, no letters, no numbers, no Apple logo, no phone mockup, no people, no animals, no emergency siren imagery, no police or military visual language. Make it suitable for an iOS app icon, recognizable at small sizes, with clean rounded-square safe margins.
```

Negative prompt:

```text
text, logo text, letters, numbers, Apple logo, phone mockup, screenshot, people, animals, weapons, police, military, emergency siren, clutter, tiny details, photorealistic scene, low contrast, flat generic wave icon
```

## 2. Launch Screen Prompt

Target use:
- App launch/loading image.
- Export final as portrait PNG or JPG.
- Recommended master size: 1290 x 2796 px, then crop/scale for target devices.
- Keep important content centered so it is safe across iPhone sizes.
- No visible text unless added later in Xcode/UI.

Prompt:

```text
Create a portrait launch screen background for NoiseRadar, an offline iPhone sound direction scanner. Show an immersive but clean dark outdoor scene at dusk with a subtle circular radar/sound-wave visualization centered in the frame. The visual should suggest listening to nearby sounds and finding their direction: soft green concentric sound waves, a minimal compass ring, and a small directional highlight. The style should be premium, calm, practical, and modern, with enough empty space in the center for the app to load over it if needed. Use deep graphite, muted forest green, and small warm red accents. No text, no app name, no buttons, no phone mockup, no people, no animals, no emergency or surveillance imagery. The final image should feel like a useful field tool, not a horror or military scene.
```

Negative prompt:

```text
text, app name, buttons, interface labels, Apple logo, phone mockup, people, animals, monsters, horror mood, police, military, weapon, emergency siren, surveillance camera, cluttered UI, bright rainbow colors, beige/brown dominated palette, unreadable busy center
```

## 3. Custom Product Page Header Prompt

Target use:
- App Store custom product page header / marketing header.
- Export as high-resolution landscape JPG or PNG.
- Recommended master size: 3840 x 1646 px.
- Leave safe space for App Store cropping.
- Should communicate the real app function without unsupported claims.

Prompt:

```text
Create a wide landscape App Store product page header for NoiseRadar, an offline sound direction and sound type detection utility for iPhone. Show a real-feeling outdoor environment such as a quiet forest path or open park at dusk, with a clean translucent radar compass visualization floating subtly over the scene. Show green sound waves coming from several directions, with one highlighted direction marked by a small red arrow or pulse. Include small abstract category hints around the radar, such as minimal icons for voice, animal, vehicle, and mechanical sound, but no text labels. The mood should be practical, calm, trustworthy, and focused on exploring nearby sounds offline. Use balanced colors: deep graphite UI elements, natural greens, and restrained red highlights. Composition should have a strong central visual signal and enough negative space for App Store cropping. No app name text, no marketing slogans, no Apple logo, no phone mockup unless it is subtle and secondary, no weapons, no military/police/emergency imagery, no scary forest mood, no surveillance imagery.
```

Negative prompt:

```text
text, slogan, app store badge, Apple logo, huge phone mockup, people close-up, animals close-up, weapon, police, military, emergency siren, surveillance camera, horror forest, fantasy scene, clutter, too many icons, stock photo look, low contrast, one-color palette, beige/brown dominated palette
```

## Notes For Final Asset Review

- Icon must be clear at 60 x 60 px.
- Launch screen must not rely on text or tiny UI details.
- Header must not imply rescue, emergency, police, military, surveillance, or guaranteed detection.
- All assets should support the App Store copy: casual offline sound direction estimates and best-effort sound type labels.
- If generated assets include accidental text, distorted symbols, logos, or identifiable copyrighted material, regenerate before using them.
